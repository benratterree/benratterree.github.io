Wi-Fi Enabled Dorm Scoreboard

September 2025–May 2026.

Overview

I built 4 custom 7-segment displays in my dorm windows to display the Aggie football score and to be used as a clock in the off season.

How it works

  • Wi-Fi-enabled Arduino controls 28 solenoid relays which switch power on and off to each segment of the display.
  • Built custom segments using LED strips
  • Installed a semi-permanent wiring system using conduit and over 500 feet of wiring

What I learned

Building stuff that runs 24/7 needs to have adequate electrical protections (fuses and/or breakers) and use permanent bonding solutions (not breadboards). One of the control circuit boards caught fire one morning and thankfully I was in the dorm to turn everything off because there were no fuses on the board and it could have been much worse. After that incident I have been super safety minded especially when it comes to electrical applications. I should've known better from the outset but I assumed low voltage = safe but that is most definitely not the case.
