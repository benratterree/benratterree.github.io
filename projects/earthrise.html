Earthrise

Published July 3, 2026 · Microsoft Store: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N0VSG1P4VKQ

Most recent update in October 2026

Overview

Earthrise is a Windows wallpaper app I published on the Microsoft Store. It combines public live satellite imagery with a computed starfield to make realistic desktop backgrounds.

It has been installed more than 150 times in 40+ countries.

How it works

  • Pulls in public live satellite imagery
  • Generates a computed starfield behind the Earth imagery
  • Combines them into high-quality, realistic wallpapers
  • Built with AI coding tools (Claude Code)
  • Published on the Microsoft Store on July 3, 2026

Challenges

The hardest part of the app development process was testing everything on multiple devices before publishing an update. I made the mistake of shipping an update once which added a feature I wanted but ended up breaking the app in other ways.

What I learned

Testing before publishing is incredibly important, especially testing for features you do not often use or for inputs you would not commonly input but which others, especially in other countries might.
