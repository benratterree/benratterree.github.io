Car Horn for My Bike with Custom Battery

2023 · Personal project

Overview

I put a car horn on my bicycle so I could honk at my friends and built a custom 18650 lithium-ion battery pack to power it.

How it works

  • Mounted a car horn on a bicycle
  • Built a custom battery pack from 18650 lithium-ion cells
  • Wired switch through the bike’s frame to reduce mess and chance of damage.
